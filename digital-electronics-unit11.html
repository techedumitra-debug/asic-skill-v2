Shift registers: storing and moving bits

A shift register is a bank of clocked storage elements. Each clock can move a bit to an adjacent stage, preserve the entire word or load several bits together.

Circuit interpretation

A four-stage register has four D flip-flops. Its capacity is four bits, irrespective of how bits enter or leave.

RELATED TOPIC · SERIAL PARALLEL

Serial and parallel data

Serial communication presents successive bits on one data line. Parallel communication presents all bits of a word on separate lines at the same time.

Transmission patternsSERIAL ↔ PARALLEL

Circuit interpretation

A common clock defines when a receiving register samples serial data. Parallel transfer reduces the number of clock cycles but requires multiple signal paths.
