Counters: the basics

A counter is a sequential circuit that advances through a specified sequence of stored bit patterns when clock events arrive. The chosen sequence determines whether it counts upward, downward, or follows a special code.

Engineering interpretation

Synchronous and asynchronous counters

In a ripple counter, the output transition of one stage clocks the next stage. In a synchronous counter all storage elements receive a common clock, while local logic decides which bits toggle.
