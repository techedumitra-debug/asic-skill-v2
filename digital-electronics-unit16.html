UNIT 16 · LESSON 02 · LOGIC FAMILIES

Bipolar and MOS logic families

TTL, ECL and I²L use bipolar junction devices; MOS and CMOS use field-effect transistors. These families differ strongly in how they switch.

Bipolar and MOS logic familiesINTERACTIVE LEARNING

RELATED TOPIC · INPUT THRESHOLD

Logic threshold and switching regionINTERACTIVE LEARNING

Engineering interpretation

An input inside an indeterminate region cannot safely be assigned 0 or 1 from the specification alone.

RELATED TOPIC · PROPAGATION DELAY

Propagation delay · tPLH and tPHLINTERACTIVE LEARNING

Fan-in and input topologyINTERACTIVE LEARNING

UNIT 16 · LESSON 08 · LOGIC FAMILIES

VIH, VIL, VOH and VOLINTERACTIVE LEARNING

Engineering interpretation

HIGH and LOW noise marginsINTERACTIVE LEARNING

Engineering interpretation

NMH = VOH(min) − VIH(min); NML = VIL(max) − VOL(max). Both must be nonnegative.
